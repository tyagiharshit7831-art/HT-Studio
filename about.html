Studio profile

I design visuals that feel sharp, cinematic, and useful.

HT Studio is the creative identity of Harshit Tyagi, a multidisciplinary artist working across graphic design, matte painting, motion graphics, VFX, animation, and cinematic video editing.

I enjoy creating posters, thumbnails, logos, business cards, social media creatives, matte-painted environments, and motion pieces that feel polished and purposeful. My goal is to make every project look clean, visually strong, and memorable from the first frame.

I am still building my creative journey, but I take each project seriously: learning, refining, experimenting, and turning ideas into visuals that can work for creators, brands, college projects, ads, reels, and digital campaigns.
